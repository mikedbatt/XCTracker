Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

XCTracker ("we," "us") is a training platform for cross country, track, and related team sports. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using XCTracker, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who this policy covers

XCTracker is used by three categories of people:

  • Athletes who log runs and complete training check-ins.
  • Coaches (head and assistant) who manage a school's roster and training.
  • Parents/guardians who follow a linked athlete's progress.

2. Information we collect

Account information

  • Name, email address, and role (athlete / coach / parent).
  • For athletes: date of birth (to verify age and compute training zones), gender.
  • For coaches: school name, location, mascot, and team join code.

Training and wellness data

  • Run records: distance, duration, heart rate (if available), perceived effort, notes, and date.
  • Daily wellness check-ins: sleep quality, leg fatigue, mood, and any reported injury/illness.
  • Weekly check-ins: a freeform message from athlete to head coach, and the coach's reply.
  • Race results and meet history.
  • Attendance records.

Strava integration (optional)

If you connect Strava, we receive your activity summaries, average heart rate, and pace data. Raw heart-rate and pace streams are processed in memory to compute training zones and are NOT permanently stored — only the derived per-zone time is saved per run.

Photos

If you attach a photo to a team feed post, the image is uploaded to our storage so teammates can view it.

Push notification tokens

If you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can send check-in reminders and coach reply notifications.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Location data (we do not track where you run).
  • Contact lists or device identifiers beyond the push token.
  • Advertising identifiers — XCTracker has no advertising.

3. How we use information

  • Show you your own training history, wellness trends, and race results.
  • Allow your coach to see your team's data so they can plan workouts and monitor health/injury risk.
  • Send notifications you've opted into (check-in reminders, coach replies, team feed posts).
  • Compute training metrics (heart-rate zones, pace zones, mileage trends).

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing.

4. Who can see your data

  • You can always see your own data.
  • Your coach (head and assistant coaches at your school) can see your runs, wellness check-ins, race results, and attendance.
  • Only your head coach can see your private weekly check-in messages. Assistant coaches do not have access to weekly check-in threads.
  • Parents/guardians linked to your account can see your training summary and the team feed. Parents cannot see your private weekly check-in messages.
  • Teammates at your school can see leaderboard rankings (miles totals) and posts you make to the team feed.

5. Children's privacy

XCTracker is used by middle-school and high-school athletes, including users under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • Athletes under 13 cannot create an account through normal signup. A parent or guardian must contact us to set up the account.
  • Athletes under 18 must provide a parent/guardian email at signup; we send a consent email to that address. The athlete can log their own runs immediately but team features (roster, feed, coach communication) unlock only after parental consent.
  • Parents/guardians can request that we delete a minor's account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Where your data lives

XCTracker uses Google Firebase for authentication, database (Firestore), file storage, and push notifications. Data is hosted in Google's secure cloud infrastructure within the United States. We use Firebase's built-in security rules to enforce the access boundaries described above.

7. Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed from our active systems within 30 days. Aggregate, anonymized statistics (e.g., total miles logged across all users) may be retained indefinitely.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we have about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Disconnect Strava at any time from your profile settings.
  • Turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be communicated in-app. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
